Dataset Overview
This is a Telecommunication Customer Churn dataset of a well known telecommunication company which contains 7,043 customer records and 21 columns covering customer demographics, services, contract types, payment methods, tenure, monthly charges, and churn status. The analysis aims to identify why customers leave, highlight revenue at risk, and recommend strategies to improve customer retention.

Key Insights
1. High churn rate: 26.5% of customers (1,869) have left the company.
2. Contract type: Month-to-month customers have the highest churn rate at 42.7%.
3. Customer tenure: Customers within their first six months have the highest churn rate, approximately 52.9%.
4. Payment method: Customers using electronic checks have the highest churn rate at 45.3%.

Recommendations
1. Improve customer retention: Introduce loyalty rewards and incentives to encourage customers to stay longer.
2. Promote long-term contracts: Offer attractive discounts and benefits to encourage customers to switch from month-to-month plans.
3. Strengthen onboarding: Provide better customer support and follow-ups during the first six months.
4. Review payment experience: Investigate why electronic-check users churn more frequently and encourage convenient alternative payment methods.
